## Prerequisites

QueryEditor is a native application bundled with everything you need. You don't have to install database CLI tools or extra runtimes on your operating system because all the required drivers (like `better-sqlite3` and `mysql2`) are baked right in.

| Platform | Support                                                    |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Windows  | x64 architecture                                           |

## Installation Process

1. Download the installer for your system from the [QueryEditor website](https://queryeditor.com).
2. Run the downloaded file and follow your OS standard instructions.
3. Launch **QueryEditor** from your applications menu.

## The Welcome Wizard

On your first run, you will be greeted by a **Welcome Wizard** designed to set up your environment in seconds.

### 1. The First Look

A warm welcome introducing you to the fast and simple philosophy of QueryEditor.

### 2. Tailoring Your Environment

Here you configure the foundation of your visual and working experience:

- **Appearance**: Choose between Light, Dark, or Auto (synced with your OS).
- **Workspace Directory**: Define the global root folder on your hard drive where your `.sql` files will reside.
- **Editor Preferences**: Toggle Line Numbers, the Minimap, or Word Wrap on the fly.

### 3. Support the Project

QueryEditor is free. In this final step, you have the option to support the ongoing development via Buy Me a Coffee before clicking **Get Started** to dive into the app.

## Your First Connection

Once the wizard is complete, the **Connections Manager** will appear.

<Image
  src={props.images.start.light}
  darksrc={props.images.start.dark}
  alt="QueryEditor Connections Manager ready to add a first database connection"
  caption="After the wizard, the Connections Manager opens so you can create your first database connection immediately."
/>

1. Click the **+** button in the top toolbar.
2. Select your preferred database engine (e.g. SQLite).
3. Fill in the required fields.
4. Press **Test** to ensure everything is working correctly.
5. Click **Save** and then double-click your newly created connection to open it.

<Image
  src={props.images.sqlite.light}
  darksrc={props.images.sqlite.dark}
  alt="SQLite connection configuration form in QueryEditor with file path input"
  caption="Select your SQLite database file to pre-fill the connection form and get connected in seconds."
/>

## Entering the Workspace

Upon opening your connection, the main **Workspace** unfolds. You are now ready to:

- Write code in the Monaco editor.
- Explore your tables in the Data Grid.
- Manage your files in the left sidebar.
- Consult the AI using `Ctrl+K`.
