## Identity and Version

The **About** window is a quick glance at the system information and the current version of your QueryEditor installation.

## What Information Can You Find Here?

- **Branding**: The official logo and application name.
- **Release Version**: The exact version number you are running (e.g. `0.1.0-alpha.1`), essential when reporting bugs.
- **Credits and Copyright**: Legal information and direct links to the official website.
- **Support the Project**: Links to Daustinn (the developer behind the project) and a "Buy me a coffee" button if you wish to contribute to continuous development.

In future updates, this screen will provide useful technical data for debugging, such as processor architecture, system locale, and the internal Node.js version of your Electron environment.
