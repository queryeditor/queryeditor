This policy covers two contexts: **(1) the QueryEditor desktop application** and **(2) this website (queryeditor.com)**.

---

## Desktop Application

### 1. Local-First

QueryEditor runs entirely on your machine. All credentials, configurations, queries, and workspace files are stored exclusively on your device. The application operates no backend server or cloud service that receives, monitors, or intercepts your data.

Credentials are encrypted at rest. Securing your operating system and controlling access to your machine remains your responsibility.

### 2. Network Connections

The application makes the following outbound connections only:

- **Update checks** — Periodic version checks against our public GitHub Releases page. GitHub handles this request per their own [Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). We do not have access to that metadata.
- **Your AI provider** (optional) — When you use the AI assistant, your prompt and any approved schema context are sent directly from your device to the provider you configured (OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google). This data never passes through QueryEditor servers.
- **Your database server** — Query execution goes directly to the database you connect to. We have no access.

No telemetry, usage metrics, crash reports, or analytics are collected by QueryEditor or its developer.

### 3. AI Features

AI features are entirely optional and require you to supply your own API key from a third-party provider. The key is stored locally and encrypted on your device. The AI reads your database schema in read-only mode; it cannot write, alter, or execute queries autonomously. Review your chosen provider's privacy policy to understand how they handle your data.

### 4. What Is Stored Locally

- Connection credentials — encrypted.
- Workspace files (`.sql`) — plain text, in a directory you choose.
- Application settings — in the OS application data directory.
- AI API keys — encrypted.

## Website (queryeditor.com)

This is a static site. When you visit:

- Standard server logs (IP address, browser user-agent, pages visited) may be recorded by our hosting provider. We do not control or access these directly.
- No analytics, tracking cookies, or fingerprinting scripts are used.
- No account or personal data collection takes place.
- Download links redirect to GitHub Releases, governed by GitHub's privacy policy.

## Changes

If data practices change, this policy will be updated and active users will be notified through the in-app updater.
